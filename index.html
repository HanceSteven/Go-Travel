Hallstatt

Austria

Hallstatt is a picturesque village in Austria known for its stunning alpine scenery, traditional architecture, and rich history centered around salt mining

Tower Bridge

London, United Kingdom

Tower Bridge is an iconic Victorian bascule and suspension bridge in London over the River Thames, famous for its neo-Gothic towers and distinctive lifting mechanism

Burj Al Arab

Dubai, United Arab Emirates

The Burj Al Arab is a 321-meter-tall luxury hotel in Dubai, UAE, built on a man-made island to resemble a billowing dhow sail. It is known for its opulent interiors decorated with 24-karat gold leaf and rare marble, a helipad for helicopter arrivals, multiple fine dining restaurants including one underwater, and its iconic, sail-like architecture.

Colosseum

Rome, Italy

The Colosseum is a massive, iconic Roman amphitheater in Rome, Italy, famous for hosting gladiatorial contests, public spectacles, and animal hunts.

Pura Ulun Danu Beratan temple

Bali, Indonesia

Pura Ulun Danu Beratan is a significant and iconic Hindu temple complex located on the shores of Lake Beratan in Bali, Indonesia. It is dedicated to the goddess Dewi Danu and is deeply connected to agriculture and water worship, with its stunning "floating" appearance on the lake making it one of Bali's most picturesque landmarks.

Eiffel Tower/Trocadéro fountains

Paris, France

The Eiffel Tower and Trocadéro Fountain are closely linked, as the Trocadéro offers one of the most famous and stunning panoramic views of the Eiffel Tower, especially from the Trocadéro Gardens and Palais de Chaillot.
